# Code of Conduct

SealedCash is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and collaborative community.

Expected behavior includes constructive feedback, respect for differing viewpoints, and care for other contributors' time and safety. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, and intentionally disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to `support@sealedcash.com`. Please include relevant context and links, and do not include credentials or sensitive user data. Reports are handled confidentially and retaliation is not tolerated.
